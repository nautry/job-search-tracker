# Daily job check

1. Open the existing tracker and compare Settings’ feed and sync timestamps with the latest successful Daily employer search run. The collector runs once a day. Use Run workflow only after a missed or failed collection when a fresh feed is needed.
2. Check Sources. A partial board can surface good jobs, but its caps limit coverage. Treat an old publication date and a missing employer date as separate cases.
3. Open Apply Next. It has up to 12 unapplied Apply candidates, generally no more than two per employer. A short queue means the search found fewer qualified candidates. Stretch and Review remain in Jobs.
4. Open the posting before submitting. Confirm actual duties, undergraduate degree, experience, May 2027 graduation and start, location, and any work authorization or clearance terms. Apply means the posting passed the automated screen. A cohort signal can leave the exact start month open.
5. Pick a Design, Test/R&D, or Mechatronics resume. Submit directly with the employer and record status, date, follow-up and notes in Jobs A:D.
6. Check Dashboard for follow-ups and offer deadlines. Record a written offer or deadline only when you have it.

Product and program flags are informational. A soft defense/dual-use target of 70–75% applies to similarly qualified candidates. Read the actual program before making your own choice.
